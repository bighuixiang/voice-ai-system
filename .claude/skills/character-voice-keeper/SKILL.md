# Character Voice Keeper

Keep recurring characters recognizable across chapters, scripts, images, and video prompts.

## First Step

Read the relevant character file under `01-角色设定/`. For group scenes, read only the characters who speak or drive the scene.

Useful files:

- `01-角色设定/主角/向德豪.md`
- `01-角色设定/魔兽/将臣.md`
- `01-角色设定/异界师父/向守一.md`
- `01-角色设定/双女主/伊莎贝拉-精灵族院长.md`
- `01-角色设定/双女主/索菲亚-校花.md`
- `01-角色设定/角色外貌描写详细版.md`

## Voice Anchors

- 向德豪: short, restrained, loyal, action-first; rarely explains himself at length.
- 将臣: talkative, sarcastic, proud, comedic contrast; silence or restraint in dark/孤狼 arcs.
- 向守一: strict but paternal; speaks like a Daoist mentor, not a modern comedian.
- 伊莎贝拉: mature, rational, elegant, occasionally sharp.
- 索菲亚: bright, direct, energetic, emotionally transparent.

## Dialogue Review

Flag:

- interchangeable voices
- modern internet slang that breaks the world
- exposition disguised as dialogue
- jokes that undercut sacrifice, grief, or major threat
- romantic beats that ignore current relationship stage

## Output

For reviews, show before/after dialogue snippets.

For new scenes, include a short note explaining how each speaker's voice was preserved.
